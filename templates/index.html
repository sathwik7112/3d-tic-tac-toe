Welcome to 3D Tic Tac Toe by Group 7!

OBJECTIVE

Get 4 of your marks in a straight line anywhere in the 3D space.

GAMEPLAY

  • You play as X, AI plays as O
  • Take turns placing your mark in any empty cell
  • Winning requires 4 in a row in any direction:
    • Horizontal rows
    • Vertical columns
    • Depth pillars
    • Any diagonal (including 3D diagonals)

DIFFICULTY LEVELS

  • EASY: AI looks 2 moves ahead
  • MEDIUM: AI looks 4 moves ahead
  • INSANE: AI looks 6 moves ahead
